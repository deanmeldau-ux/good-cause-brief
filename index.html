2. Founder Story "It's Imprinted in My DNA"

Position: After hero, before "The Crisis" section

Heading

IT'S IMPRINTED IN MY DNA.

Body Copy

I started Good Cause because I believe it is our duty to protect animals. They have no say in the circumstances they're born into. They can't ask for help. We have to do it for them.

I come from a family of animal rescuers. This wasn't a career choice it's who I am. And I believe, without question, that all creatures are equal.

What I've seen up close is this: the organisations doing the hardest work on the ground are often so passionate, so committed, that they run themselves into the ground just to keep going. They shouldn't have to spend their energy worrying about how they keep their doors open. They should be doing what they do best saving lives.

That's where Good Cause comes in. We exist to stand beside these organisations. To showcase their work, grow their reach, and help fund their operations so they can focus on the animals, and nothing else.

Dean Meldau, Founder & Chairperson, Good Cause NPO

THE ORGANISATIONS WE STAND BESIDE.

Intro Copy

Good Cause doesn't work in isolation. We stand beside the rescuers, shelters, and welfare organisations doing the hardest work in South Africa — amplifying their campaigns, connecting them with donors, and helping them reach the people who care.

Here are some of the organisations we are proud to support.

Partner Cards — Ready to Build

One card per organisation. Each shows name, location, what they do, and Good Cause's role. Logos to be supplied by Good Cause Team.

CAPE OF GOOD HOPE DBV SPCA
Cape Town

South Africa's oldest animal welfare organisation, founded in 1872. They respond to approximately 55,000 animal cruelty alerts per year, operate four mobile clinics, and run specialised units for horses, feral cats, wildlife, and farm animals across the Cape Peninsula.

WOLLIES ANIMAL SHELTER NPC
Pretoria North

A women-owned, no-kill shelter caring for approximately 450 dogs and 100 cats at any one time — keeping every animal until it finds a permanent home. They also operate a community veterinary clinic offering heavily subsidised sterilisations and vaccinations to pensioners and low-income households.

TIN CAN TOWN
Blikkiesdorp, Cape Town

A women-led NPO working in one of Cape Town's most poverty-stricken informal settlements. Founded in 2012, they run monthly outreach days providing free food, treatment, sterilisations, and vaccinations to community animals while educating residents to become volunteer carers in their own neighbourhoods.

PAW PRINTS RESCUE AND REHOMING
Botha's Hill, KwaZulu-Natal

A registered no-kill NPO that has rehomed over 6,000 dogs since founding in 2015. Operating entirely on volunteers and donations with no paid staff, they rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and neglected puppies and dogs from a 2-hectare property in the KZN Midlands.

ADORA-BULL TERRIER RESCUE AND REHABILITATION
Vereeniging, Gauteng

South Africa's first and only independent Bull Terrier NPO, founded by a qualified animal behaviourist in 2012. They rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome Bull Terriers nationally — directly challenging the breed misconceptions that lead to abandonment and neglect.

CHERYL LYN'S RESCUE ORGANISATION
Cape Town

A 24/7 foster-based rescue operating across Tableview, Blouberg, Milnerton, and surrounding informal settlements. Founded in 2015, they rescue and rehome neglected and abused dogs and cats entirely through a dedicated foster network, with no physical shelter.

12. Terms and Conditions + Privacy Policy

Scope: Two new pages + footer links

Why This Is Needed

Good Cause collects donor information, email addresses, and processes payments via PayFast. POPIA (South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act) requires a published Privacy Policy. The Terms and Conditions protect Good Cause legally from misuse of the site and clarify the nature of donations.

Pages to Create

  • goodcausecares.com/privacy-policy — covers data collection, how donor info is used, POPIA compliance, cookie policy, third-party processors (PayFast)
  • goodcausecares.com/terms — covers website use, donation terms, no-refund policy, NPO liability limits, intellectual property
