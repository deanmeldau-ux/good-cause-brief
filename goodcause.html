Who We Are

"It's imprinted in my DNA."

I started Good Cause because I believe it is our duty to protect animals. They have no say in the circumstances they're born into. They can't ask for help. We have to do it for them.

I come from a family of animal rescuers. This wasn't a career choice it's who I am. And I believe, without question, that all creatures are equal.

What I've seen up close is this: the organisations doing the hardest work on the ground are often so passionate, so committed, that they run themselves into the ground just to keep going. They shouldn't have to spend their energy worrying about how they keep their doors open. They should be doing what they do best saving lives.

That's where Good Cause comes in. We exist to stand beside these organisations. To showcase their work, grow their reach, and help fund their operations so they can focus on the animals, and nothing else.

Dean Meldau, Founder and Chairperson, Good Cause NPO

Countless domestic animals are suffering in silence. Overcrowded shelters, rampant breeding, and a lack of resources create a desperate situation. This is a crisis of compassion, and every moment counts.

Our Mission

DIRECT ACTION.
TANGIBLE RELIEF.

Good Cause doesn't work in isolation. We stand beside rescuers, shelters, and welfare organisations amplifying their reach and funding their work.

SAVE AND HEAL

When an animal is hurt, abandoned, or terrified, we step in with our rescue partners to ensure they receive the medical care, safety, and healing they deserve.

HOME AND HOPE

We work with compassionate shelters and rescues to place animals into families who will love them for life while using our voice to fight for stronger laws and better protection.

MOBILISE AND MULTIPLY

We use storytelling, media, and public mobilisation to highlight urgent animal issues and connect thousands of supporters with the organisations fighting on the ground.

THE ORGANISATIONS
WE SUPPORT.

Good Cause doesn't work in isolation. We stand beside the rescuers, shelters, and welfare organisations doing the hardest work in South Africa amplifying their campaigns, connecting them with donors, and helping them reach the people who care.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
CAPE OF GOOD HOPE DBV SPCA
Cape Town

South Africa's oldest animal welfare organisation, founded in 1872. They respond to approximately 55,000 animal cruelty alerts per year and operate four mobile clinics serving communities across the Cape Peninsula.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
WOLLIES ANIMAL SHELTER NPC
Pretoria North

A women-owned, no-kill shelter caring for approximately 450 dogs and 100 cats keeping every animal until it finds a permanent home. They also run a community veterinary clinic offering heavily subsidised care.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
TIN CAN TOWN
Blikkiesdorp, Cape Town

A women-led NPO working in one of Cape Town's most poverty-stricken informal settlements. Monthly outreach days provide free food, treatment, sterilisations, and vaccinations to community animals.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
PAW PRINTS RESCUE AND REHOMING
Bothas Hill, KwaZulu-Natal

A registered no-kill NPO that has rehomed over 6,000 dogs since 2015. Operating entirely on volunteers and donations, they rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and neglected dogs from their KZN property.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
ADORA-BULL TERRIER RESCUE
Vereeniging, Gauteng

South Africa's first and only independent Bull Terrier NPO, founded by a qualified animal behaviourist. They rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome Bull Terriers nationally, fighting the breed misconceptions that lead to abandonment.

REPLACE WITH ORG LOGO
CHERYL LYN'S RESCUE ORGANISATION
Cape Town

A 24/7 foster-based rescue operating across Tableview, Blouberg, Milnerton, and surrounding informal settlements. Founded in 2015, they rescue and rehome neglected and abused animals entirely through a dedicated foster network.
